# Zoom integration

The Zoom integration connects your Zoom account to Notifyy for **meeting management**, **webinar synchronization**, and **user authorization**. Setup uses **Zoom OAuth 2.0**—Notifyy operates strictly within the scopes you approve during authorization.

---

## Application overview

This integration enables:

| Area | Capabilities |
|------|----------------|
| **Meetings** | Retrieve, create, update, and delete Zoom meetings |
| **Users** | Retrieve user information and sync host details |
| **Webinars** | Retrieve webinar information; create and update webinars |

**Data synchronization:**

- **Initial sync** runs immediately after connection
- **Incremental sync** runs automatically at defined intervals
- **Zoom webhooks** provide near real-time updates when configured

---

## Prerequisites

- A valid **Zoom account**
- Permission to **install and authorize** Zoom applications
- Access to the **Notifyy** application platform

<TipBox>
  Confirm with your Zoom admin that you can authorize third-party apps before starting OAuth.
</TipBox>

---

## Step 1: Initiate Zoom connection

1. Log in to the **Notifyy** application.
2. Navigate to **Integrations** → **Platforms**.

<img src="/docs/integrations/zoom/screenshots/zoom-1.png" alt="Notifyy sidebar with Integrations menu and Platforms submenu" className="mx-auto rounded-lg border border-gray-200 shadow-sm my-6" />

3. Locate the **Zoom** integration card under available platforms.
4. Click **Connect** on the Zoom integration card.

<img src="/docs/integrations/zoom/screenshots/zoom-2.png" alt="Notifyy Integrations Platforms page with Zoom card and Connect button" className="mx-auto rounded-lg border border-gray-200 shadow-sm my-6" />

<DocsVideo src="/docs/integrations/zoom/recordings/integration-zoom.mp4" />

The Zoom card shows **Video Conferencing** with **API**, **Webhook**, and **OAuth** tags.

---

## Step 2: Authorization process

After you click **Connect**, Notifyy redirects to the **Zoom OAuth** authorization screen.

### Sign in to Zoom

On the Zoom sign-in page, authenticate using your preferred method:

- **Zoom account credentials** — Email address and password
- **SSO** (Single Sign-On)
- **Apple**
- **Google**
- **Facebook**
- **Microsoft**

<img src="/docs/integrations/zoom/screenshots/zoom-3.png" alt="Zoom OAuth sign-in page with credentials and social sign-in options" className="mx-auto rounded-lg border border-gray-200 shadow-sm my-6" />

### Review scopes and authorize

- **Requested scopes** are displayed for review
- Authorization is completed by selecting **Authorize**
- Upon approval, the system redirects back to Notifyy with an authorization code (handled automatically)

<img src="/docs/integrations/zoom/screenshots/zoom-4.png" alt="Notifyy Platforms page showing Zoom Connected after OAuth authorization" className="mx-auto rounded-lg border border-gray-200 shadow-sm my-6" />

<WarningBox>
  Denying a required scope blocks meeting or webinar actions that depend on it. Disconnect and connect again, approving the full scope set, to restore access.
</WarningBox>

---

## Step 3: Connection establishment

When authorization succeeds:

- The Zoom account is **securely linked**
- **OAuth tokens** are generated and stored securely (encrypted)
- **Initial synchronization** is triggered

Return to **Integrations → Platforms** and confirm the Zoom card shows **Connected**.

---

## Meeting management

- Retrieve meetings from Zoom
- **Create** meetings
- **Update** meetings
- **Delete** meetings

---

## User management

- Retrieve **user information** from Zoom
- **Sync host details** for scheduling workflows

---

## Webinar management

- Retrieve **webinar information**
- **Create** and **update** webinars

---

## Requested scopes (example)

| Scope | Purpose |
|-------|---------|
| `meeting:read` | Read and list meetings |
| `meeting:write` | Create, update, delete meetings |
| `webinar:read` | Read webinar data |
| `webinar:write` | Create and update webinars |
| `user:read` | Read user and host information |

---

## Security and data handling

- All API communication uses **HTTPS**
- OAuth tokens are stored in **encrypted** form
- Data access is limited strictly to **authorized scopes**

---

## Token lifecycle management

- **Access tokens** refresh automatically prior to expiration
- **Refresh failures** trigger re-authentication—click **Connect** on the Zoom card again

---

## Disconnection process

Navigate to **Integrations** → **Platforms** and open the Zoom integration card.

After successfully integrating Zoom, the card displays a **Connected** status and provides two options:

- **Open configuration** — opens Zoom integration settings and configuration details
- **Disconnect** — removes the Zoom integration from your Notifyy account

<img src="/docs/integrations/zoom/screenshots/zoom-5.png" alt="Zoom Connected card with Open configuration and Disconnect buttons" className="mx-auto rounded-lg border border-gray-200 shadow-sm my-6" />

Click **Disconnect** to remove the Zoom integration from the account.

<img src="/docs/integrations/zoom/screenshots/zoom-6.png" alt="Zoom disconnected success notification with Connect button restored on Zoom card" className="mx-auto rounded-lg border border-gray-200 shadow-sm my-6" />

Notifyy **deletes token data** and **stops synchronization**. Meetings in Zoom are not deleted—only the Notifyy link ends.

---

## Error handling and reliability

- **Automatic retry** mechanisms for transient failures
- **Token expiration** handled via the refresh flow
- **Failures** are logged and monitored
