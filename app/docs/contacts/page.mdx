# Contact Management

The **Contact** object in Notifyy CRM stores and organizes customer information in one place—names, communication details, ownership, and the data you need for day-to-day operations. Contacts are the base record type for most customer-facing work in Notifyy.

Well-maintained contacts support **segments**, **workflows**, **WhatsApp communication**, **campaigns**, and reporting. This guide covers how to create contacts, update them quickly, filter lists, and recover deleted records.

Go to **CRM → Contacts** to open the contact workspace.

<img src="/docs/contacts/list-view.png" alt="Notifyy CRM contacts listing with search, filters, and Add Contact" className="mx-auto rounded-lg border border-gray-200 shadow-sm my-6" />

## When should you use contacts?

Use the Contact module when you need to:

- Store **customer or lead details** your team will message or follow up on.
- Keep **email and phone** in a consistent format for WhatsApp and email channels.
- Assign **contact owners** for sales or support accountability.
- Build **segments and audiences** from contact properties (see [Segments](/docs/segments)).
- Import records in bulk (see [Import Data](/docs/import)).

Contacts are individual people—not companies. Link companies or deals through related objects when your CRM model uses them.

## 01. Create a contact

You can add contacts one at a time with **Add Contact**. The creation panel opens on the **right side** of the screen so you can keep the list visible while entering details.

1. Go to **CRM → Contacts**.
2. Click **Add Contact**.
3. Enter customer details in the panel (name, email, phone, and any other fields your workspace exposes).
4. Save the contact with **Create**, or use **Create & add another** to enter multiple records in a row.

<img src="/docs/contacts/create-form.png" alt="New Contact form with first name, email, and phone fields" className="mx-auto rounded-lg border border-gray-200 shadow-sm my-6" />

The form marks required fields (for example, **First name** and contact details). Optional fields such as gender, order ID, or date of birth appear when your workspace includes them.

### Customize form fields

Admins can change which fields appear on the create form via **Customize form fields** in the New Contact panel.

<img src="/docs/contacts/edit-contact-form.png" alt="Edit Contact form builder to add and reorder fields" className="mx-auto rounded-lg border border-gray-200 shadow-sm my-6" />

### Validation rules

Notifyy requires enough information to identify and reach the person. Creation succeeds only when these rules are met:

| Rule | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Name | **First name** is required. |
| Reachability | At least one of **email** or **phone number** must be provided. |

**Valid combinations**

- **First name** + **email address**
- **First name** + **phone number**
- **First name** + **email** + **phone** (recommended for WhatsApp and email outreach)

**Invalid examples**

- Email only, with no first name.
- First name only, with no email and no phone.
- Empty submission.

If validation fails, the form shows an appropriate message and **does not save** until the required combination is complete.

<WarningBox>
  Use **E.164-style phone numbers** with country code where possible (for example, +91 for India). Incorrect formats cause delivery and segment matching issues later.
</WarningBox>

<TipBox>
  For WhatsApp-first teams, capture **phone** and **first name** on every contact you plan to message. Email alone is not enough for WhatsApp campaigns.
</TipBox>

## 02. Inline edit

**Inline edit** lets you change a single field **directly in the contacts table** without opening the full record page. This is useful for quick corrections—updating an email, owner, city, or status while reviewing a list.

### How it works

1. On the contacts listing, locate the field you want to change.
2. Click the cell or use the inline edit control for that column.
3. Enter the new value and confirm (save on blur or explicit save, depending on your UI).
4. The grid refreshes with the updated value.

**Example:** A user notices a typo in an email address on the list view, clicks the **Email** cell in the table, fixes it, and continues working without opening the full New Contact form.

Inline edits happen in the same **contacts table** shown in the list view—use row checkboxes only when you intend bulk actions, not when editing a single cell.

### Expected behavior

- The new value is **saved immediately** after you confirm the edit.
- The **contacts grid** shows the updated data without a full page reload in most cases.
- If save fails (validation or permissions), the cell should show an error and **revert or allow retry**—the old value must not silently disappear.

Inline edit is best for **one field on one row**. For many fields on many rows, use **bulk edit** instead.

## 03. Bulk edit

**Bulk edit** updates **multiple contacts at once** with the same change—ideal when you are assigning an owner, updating a lifecycle stage, or setting a shared property after an import or event.

1. On **CRM → Contacts**, select the checkboxes for the records you want to update.
2. Open the bulk action menu (for example, **Bulk edit** or **Edit**).
3. Choose the field(s) to change and enter the new value(s).
4. Apply the change and confirm if prompted.

**Example:** After a trade show, select forty new contacts and set **Contact owner** to the inside-sales rep who will follow up.

<DocsVideo src="/docs/contacts/bulk-edit.mp4" />

### Expected behavior

- Changes apply to **all selected records** in one operation.
- A summary or success message should indicate how many contacts were updated.
- If some rows fail validation, the UI should report **which records failed** rather than failing silently for the whole batch.

<WarningBox>
  Bulk edit cannot override **required field rules** on individual records. If a change would leave a contact without a valid email/phone combination where your workspace enforces it, those rows may be skipped or rejected.
</WarningBox>

## 04. Filters

**Filters** narrow the contacts list to records that match conditions you define—so you can work on a subset without exporting or building a segment first.

### Common filter examples

| Goal | Example filter |
|---|---|
| Location | **City** is equal to **Chennai** |
| Ownership | **Contact owner** is a specific user |
| Recency | **Created date** is within **this month** |
| Segment-style logic | Custom field **Customer tier** equals **Premium** |

1. Open the filter builder on the contacts page.
2. Add one or more conditions (field, operator, value).
3. Apply filters to refresh the grid.
4. Clear or adjust filters when you need the full list again.

Use the **Filters** control on the contacts toolbar (next to **Edit columns** and **Sort**) to open the filter builder.

<img src="/docs/contacts/filters.png" alt="Contact filters applied on the contacts listing" className="mx-auto rounded-lg border border-gray-200 shadow-sm my-6" />

### Expected behavior

- You can apply **multiple filters** at the same time (typically combined with AND logic unless your UI offers OR groups).
- You can **modify** active filters without starting over.
- You can **clear all filters** to return to the unfiltered contact list.
- Filtered views are for **working lists** in CRM; for reusable campaign audiences, save criteria as a [Segment](/docs/segments).

<TipBox>
  Use filters for operational triage (today’s leads, one owner’s queue). Use **segments** when the same audience will be reused in campaigns or automation.
</TipBox>

## 05. Recently deleted contacts

When you delete a contact, Notifyy moves it to **Recently Deleted** instead of removing it permanently right away. This gives you a window to **restore** mistakes or **purge** records you no longer need.

1. Open **CRM → Contacts**.
2. Click **Recently deleted** on the toolbar (trash icon).
3. Select one or more deleted records if you need bulk actions.
4. Click **Restore** to move records back to the active list, or **Delete permanently** to remove them for good.

<img src="/docs/contacts/recently-deleted.png" alt="Recently deleted contacts with Restore and Delete permanently actions" className="mx-auto rounded-lg border border-gray-200 shadow-sm my-6" />

### Available actions

| Action | Result |
|---|---|
| **Restore contact** | Record returns to the **active contacts** list with its data intact. |
| **Permanently delete** | Record is removed for good and **cannot be recovered**. |

### Expected behavior

- **Restored** contacts appear again in the main contacts grid and behave like any active record (filters, segments, and ownership apply as before).
- **Permanently deleted** contacts are gone from Recently Deleted and from active lists—they should not sync back from integrations unless created again as new records.

<WarningBox>
  Permanent delete is irreversible. Confirm you are not removing contacts still referenced in live segments, campaigns, or compliance holds before using **Permanently delete**.
</WarningBox>

## Summary

Contacts are the core customer records in Notifyy CRM. Create them with **first name** plus **email or phone**, use **inline edit** for quick single-field fixes, **bulk edit** for batch updates, **filters** to focus the list, and **Recently Deleted** to restore or purge records safely. Accurate contact data keeps WhatsApp messaging, segments, and team ownership reliable as you scale.
