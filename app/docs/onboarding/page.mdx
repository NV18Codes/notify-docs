# Customer Onboarding

This guide covers **Meta (WhatsApp Business API) onboarding** for businesses using Notifyy. You will prepare mandatory prerequisites first, then complete Notifyy’s embedded signup flow to link your Facebook Business Portfolio, WhatsApp Business Account (WABA), and phone number.

Approval typically takes **2–7 business days** once documents and names match across Meta, your website, and GST records. Some steps finish in hours; business verification can take longer.

👉 [Access Notifyy Platform](https://www.notifyy.io/)

<TipBox>
  Prepare everything in the **Prerequisites** section before you click **Login with Facebook** in Notifyy. Missing or mismatched documents are the most common cause of delays.
</TipBox>

---

## Overview

To use WhatsApp Business API through Notifyy, Meta requires:

- A **dedicated phone number** not active on consumer WhatsApp apps.
- A **Meta Business Manager** account with accurate legal details.
- A **live business website** and **domain-matched email**.
- **GST or equivalent business registration** for Indian entities.
- A compliant **WhatsApp display name** aligned with your brand.

After prerequisites are ready, you complete Notifyy’s **embedded signup** (Facebook login → portfolio → WABA → number → permissions). Post-connection steps in Meta include **payment setup** and **business verification** in WhatsApp Manager.

---

## Onboarding prerequisites

Complete these items **before** starting Notifyy onboarding. Requirements follow Meta policy for the **India region** (May 2026) and may change when Meta updates its policies.

### 1. Phone number (new / dedicated)

The number registered for WhatsApp Business API must:

- **Not** be linked to any existing WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business app account.
- Be **released** from WhatsApp apps first (delete the app account on that number if it was in use).
- Receive **OTP** via SMS or voice call during registration.
- Work as an **Indian mobile** or supported **VoIP** number per Meta rules.
- Remain on the **API only** after registration—it cannot return to the regular WhatsApp consumer app.

<WarningBox>
  A **brand-new SIM** is strongly recommended to avoid migration conflicts and failed number registration.
</WarningBox>

### 2. Facebook (Meta) Business Manager account

API access requires a Meta Business Manager:

- Use a **personal Facebook account** as the admin/owner who will run onboarding.
- Create Business Manager at [business.facebook.com](https://business.facebook.com).
- Enter **business name, address, and phone** exactly as on legal documents.
- Assign **admin roles** to teammates who will manage WhatsApp and verification.

<WarningBox>
  Without a Business Manager—and ideally **business verification**—WhatsApp API access stays limited. You can apply for verification while onboarding with Notifyy.
</WarningBox>

### 3. Business website

Meta expects a **public, live** website:

- No login walls or permanent “under construction” pages.
- **Legal business name** in the footer, matching GST/legal documents.
- Clear description of **products or services**.
- **Contact details and business address** visible.
- A Facebook Page or Instagram profile may help during signup, but a **live website** is required for full business verification.

<TipBox>
  No site yet? Use a one-page site (Google Sites, Wix, Carrd) and put your **legal name in the footer** before you submit verification.
</TipBox>

### 4. Business email (domain-matched)

Meta uses email to confirm business ownership:

- Use **name@yourbusiness.com** on the same domain as your website.
- **Free providers** (Gmail, Yahoo, Outlook) are not accepted for verification.
- Keep access to this inbox—Meta may send verification or action-required notices.

### 5. GST certificate / business registration

For Indian businesses, submit official registration:

**Primary (recommended)**

- **GST certificate** (most reliable for Meta India)
- **MSME / Udyam** registration
- **Certificate of incorporation** (Pvt. Ltd.)

**Supporting**

- Bank statement with business name and address
- Utility bill for address proof
- Shop & Establishment certificate (may be rejected—prefer GST when available)

**Document quality**

- Colour scan, full document visible.
- **Legal name** must match Facebook Business Manager **exactly**.
- **Address** must match Meta Business Manager.

<WarningBox>
  **Aadhaar and PAN alone are not accepted** as business proof. Use a business registration document.
</WarningBox>

### Critical name-matching rule

The legal name must be **identical** on:

1. GST / registration document  
2. Facebook Business Manager  
3. Website footer  

Even small differences (**Pvt Ltd** vs **Private Limited**) can cause rejection. Fix Business Manager first, then re-submit documents.

### 6. WhatsApp Business display name

Customers see this name in WhatsApp. Meta rules:

- Must reflect your **real brand or legal trade name**.
- Must **not** be a generic phrase or marketing slogan (e.g. “Best Deals India”).
- Must align with **website and legal documents**.
- Meta may **re-review** the display name as messaging limits increase.

<TipBox>
  Display name review often takes **24–48 hours** after business verification progresses.
</TipBox>

### Quick reference checklist

| Requirement | What to prepare |
|---|---|
| New mobile number | Fresh SIM, not on WhatsApp apps; OTP-capable |
| Facebook account | Personal account to admin Business Manager |
| Meta Business Manager | Created at business.facebook.com; details match GST |
| Business website | Live site; legal name in footer; matches domain email |
| Business email | Domain email (e.g. you@yourdomain.com) |
| GST / registration | Legal name matches Business Manager exactly |
| Display name | Brand name only—no slogans or generic terms |

### Expected approval timeline

| Step | Estimated time |
|---|---|
| Meta business verification | 2–4 business days |
| WABA creation and number setup | Same day (a few hours) |
| Display name approval | 24–48 hours |
| Total (approx.) | **3–10 business days** |

Template and messaging approvals happen **after** onboarding in separate WhatsApp Manager workflows—not during this connection guide.

## Meta WhatsApp API onboarding via Notifyy

When prerequisites are ready, follow the embedded signup flow in Notifyy.

<WarningBox>
  If the number already has **WhatsApp** or **WhatsApp Business** installed, **remove or migrate** that account before onboarding. Meta often fails number registration when the line is still active on the consumer app.
</WarningBox>

---

## Step 1: Log in via Notifyy

1. Open the Notifyy onboarding page for your workspace.
2. Click **Login with Facebook**.
3. Grant the **permissions** Meta requests for Business Portfolio and WhatsApp setup.

Use the same Facebook user who admins your Meta Business Manager.

## Step 2: Select or create a business portfolio

1. Choose an **existing Meta Business Account** or create a new **Business Portfolio**.
2. Enter **Business name**, **business email**, **category**, **website URL**, **country**, and **time zone**.
3. Confirm details match your GST and website footer before continuing.

## Step 3: Create a WhatsApp Business Account (WABA)

Meta creates your **WhatsApp Business Account**, **display name**, and **WhatsApp business profile**.

1. Review the **display name**—it must match GST, Business Manager, and your website.
2. Complete profile fields Meta requires for your category and region.

## Step 4: Add your WhatsApp number

1. Enter a **fresh mobile number** not in use on WhatsApp apps.
2. Choose **SMS** or **voice call** for OTP.
3. Complete **OTP verification** on that device or line.

## Step 5: Complete embedded signup

1. Review the **permission summary** linking Facebook Business, WABA, and Notifyy.
2. Click **Continue** or **Finish** to complete embedded signup.
3. Wait until Notifyy shows the workspace as **connected**.

## Step 6: Open WhatsApp Manager

1. In [Meta Business Suite](https://business.facebook.com), open **All tools**.
2. Launch **WhatsApp Manager** for the portfolio you connected.
3. Confirm your **WABA**, **phone number**, and **display name** appear correctly.

## Step 7: Add a payment method

WhatsApp Business API billing requires a valid payment method in Meta.

1. In WhatsApp Manager, go to **Settings → Payment configuration** (or **Billing**).
2. Add a **credit or debit card** accepted by Meta for your country.
3. Save billing details before scaling message volume.

## Step 8: Verify your business

1. Go to **Business settings → Security center** (or **Business verification**).
2. Click **Start verification**.

## Step 9: Wait for Meta verification

- Review status in **Security center** or WhatsApp Manager.
- Respond to **Action required** items Meta emails or surfaces in the UI.
- Typical range: **a few minutes to 2–3 business days**; complex cases may take longer.

## Step 10: Finish setup in Notifyy

When Meta shows a connected WABA and verification is submitted or approved:

1. Return to Notifyy and confirm **WhatsApp** status is **Connected** with no blocking errors.
2. Note your **phone number ID** and **WABA** in settings if your team needs them for support.
3. Proceed to operational guides when you are ready—for example [Connect WhatsApp](/docs/connect-whatsapp) checks and [Verify business](/docs/verify-business) follow-ups.

## Important notes

- **Number conflict:** Remove WhatsApp / WhatsApp Business from the line before API registration.
- **Name mismatch:** Align GST, Business Manager, and website footer before uploading documents.
- **Policy changes:** Meta may update requirements; check Meta Business Help Center for the latest India guidance.
- **Notifyy support:** If embedded signup fails after prerequisites are correct, contact [support@notifyy.io](mailto:support@notifyy.io) with screenshots and your Business Manager ID.

---

## Summary

Prepare a dedicated number, verified Business Manager, live website, domain email, GST-aligned documents, and an approved display name—then complete Notifyy’s **Login with Facebook** embedded signup through WABA and number verification. Finish payment and business verification in **WhatsApp Manager**, wait for Meta approval, and confirm **Connected** status in Notifyy before moving to day-to-day product configuration.
